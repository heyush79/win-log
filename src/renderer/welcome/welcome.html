Welcome to Win Log

  1. Once a day, Win Log asks: “What did you do today?”
  2. You type a line or two. It takes a few seconds.
  3. At review time, you export your wins and you are ready.

Where to find it

Win Log lives in the menu bar, at the top right of your screen. Look for the star. Click it to add a win or see your wins.

Win Log lives in the system tray, at the bottom right of your screen. Look for the star. If you do not see it, click the small arrow (^) to show hidden icons. You can drag the star onto the taskbar to keep it in view.
