What Win Log does

A daily question

At 6 pm on weekdays, Win Log asks “What did you do today?” You can change the time and the days. If your computer was asleep, it asks when you come back that day. It does not ask on a day when you already added a win.

You can also add a win at any time, from the menu or with a keyboard shortcut.

All your wins in one place

“My Wins” shows your wins week by week, newest first. Search them, filter by tag, and edit or delete any win.

To tag a win, add a word with # as you type, like #bugfix.

Ready for review time

Pick a date range, like “Last 6 months”. Get a Markdown list grouped by tag, or plain text for HR tools that do not take Markdown.

Check the preview, then copy it or save it as a file.

How it works

  1. Install Win Log

    On a Mac, open the .dmg file and drag Win Log to Applications. On Windows, run the setup file. You do not need admin rights.

    Win Log is not code-signed yet, so your computer shows a warning the first time you open it. Follow the steps for Mac or the steps for Windows.

  2. Find the star

    Win Log has no main window. Everything starts from its star icon: in the menu bar at the top right of your Mac screen, or in the system tray at the bottom right on Windows.

    Can’t see it? See I can’t find the icon.

  3. Write down your wins

    When Win Log asks what you did today, type one win per line. To tag a win, add a word with #:

    Fixed the login bug #bugfix

    To add a win at any other time, press Control+Option+W on a Mac or Ctrl+Alt+Shift+W on Windows.

  4. Export at review time

    Choose “Export…” in the menu and pick a date range. Check the preview, then click “Copy to clipboard” or “Save as file…”.

Your wins stay on your computer

Win Log saves your wins in plain files on your own computer. The author of Win Log cannot see them.

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No server. Win Log never sends your wins anywhere.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no ads.
  • The app never connects to the internet. It has no auto-updater.

Links in the app, like “Check for updates”, open in your browser, and only when you click them.

About this website

This website has no trackers and no analytics. To show the newest download links, it asks GitHub’s API for the latest release. GitHub hosts this site and the downloads, so GitHub can see these requests, like any web host.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my wins saved?

On your computer, in plain JSON files in the Win Log data folder:

  • Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/Win Log/
  • Windows: %APPDATA%\Win Log\

wins.json holds your wins and settings.json holds your settings. To open the folder, choose “Settings…” > “Your data” > “Open data folder”.

How do I back up my wins?

Win Log already makes backups. Before the first change of each day, it copies wins.json into the backups folder inside the data folder. It keeps the newest 14 copies.

To keep a copy somewhere else, open “Settings…” > “Your data”:

  • Click “Export backup…” to save all your wins in one .json file.
  • Or tick “Also save daily backups to a folder I choose” and pick a folder. Pick one inside your iCloud Drive, Google Drive or OneDrive to get a synced copy of your backups for free. Win Log keeps the newest 14 copies from this computer there and never touches your other files. Your live data always stays in the normal data folder.

You can also copy wins.json yourself.

How do I move to a new computer?
  1. On the old computer, choose “Settings…” > “Your data” > “Export backup…”.
  2. Install Win Log on the new computer.
  3. On the new computer, choose “Settings…” > “Your data” > “Import backup…” and pick the file.

Win Log shows a summary before it imports anything. It only adds wins that are not already there, and it never overwrites a win you have.

Another way: quit Win Log on both computers, then copy wins.json into the data folder on the new one.

macOS says Apple could not verify Win Log, or that it is from an “unidentified developer”

Win Log is not code-signed yet, because signing costs money. So macOS warns you the first time you open it. Only follow these steps for a file you downloaded from this page or from the Releases page.

  • macOS 13 and 14: in Finder, open Applications. Control-click (or right-click) Win Log, choose “Open”, then click “Open” again.
  • macOS 15 Sequoia and later: try to open Win Log once, then click “Done” in the warning (not “Move to Trash”). Then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click “Open Anyway”. You may need to confirm with your password.
  • If macOS says Win Log “is damaged”: do not move it to the Trash. Close the warning, open Terminal (in Applications > Utilities), run this command, then open Win Log again. 
    xattr -cr "/Applications/Win Log.app"
Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

Win Log is not code-signed yet, so Microsoft Defender SmartScreen shows this warning. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. Only do this for a file you downloaded from this page or from the Releases page.

Your browser may also say that the file is not commonly downloaded. If it does, choose to keep the file.

I can’t find the icon

Win Log has no main window. Look for its star icon:

  • Mac: in the menu bar at the top right of the screen. If the menu bar is full, macOS hides some icons, for example behind the camera notch. Quit another menu bar app to make room.
  • Windows: in the system tray at the bottom right of the screen. It may be hidden behind the ^ arrow (“Show hidden icons”). Drag it onto the taskbar to keep it visible.

Still not sure? Open Win Log again from Applications on a Mac, or from the Start menu on Windows. If it was already running, the “Add a win” window opens. If it was not, it starts.

Closing a Win Log window does not quit the app.

How do I uninstall Win Log?
  • Mac: choose “Quit” in the Win Log menu, then drag Win Log from Applications to the Trash.
  • Windows: open Settings > Apps > Installed apps (Apps & features on Windows 10), find Win Log and choose “Uninstall”.

Your wins stay in the data folder until you delete that folder. See where your wins are saved.

Is it free?

Yes. Win Log is free and open source, under the MIT license. There is no account, no subscription and no ads. You can read all of its code on GitHub.

Why are there no automatic updates?

An auto-updater has to connect to the internet, and Win Log never does. To check for a new version, choose “About Win Log” > “Check for updates”. This opens the Releases page in your browser.

To update, choose “Quit” in the Win Log menu. Then download the new version and install it the same way as the first time. Your wins stay where they are. You may see the first-time warning again, because Win Log is not code-signed yet.
